Photobook layouts for a 4:5 page

__COUNT__ single-page templates from 1 to 6 photos, each count shown with and without captions. Everything is drawn to an 8 × 10 in page (20 × 25 cm), the same proportion as 16 × 20 in prints and 1080 × 1350 px portrait posts. Tap any page for exact positions.

Cap a page at 6 photos. Design most pages for 1 to 4.

The brands people rate highest, like Artifact Uprising and Mixbook, lean on restrained pages: one hero photo with a few supporting shots. At 6 photos the smallest tile on an 8 × 10 page is still about 2 in, big enough for faces. If a moment needs more, split it across two pages.

1–3 Impactopeners, portraits, pauses, stories 4–6 Storyevents, recaps, the everyday workhorse
