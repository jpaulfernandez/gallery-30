WORKFLOW_PLAN [1.2](../WORKFLOW_PLAN.md#12-display-targets-what-display-can-be) (W4.4). Size: 2 days. Depends on: 13.

**Goal.** The book as a print-ready PDF: spreads or single pages, bleed and trim marks, 300 dpi, through the
headless renderer the CLI already runs.

## Decisions

- The `print` output variant holds settings only: `{ source: 'book' | outputId, imposition: 'pages' | 'spreads', bleedMm, marks, dpi, paper }`;
  the pages are the book's.
- Rendering: a CLI subcommand `photobook print <project> --output <id> --out file.pdf` using Playwright `page.pdf`
  at the trim size plus bleed, one page per PDF page, the shared `<Page>` scaled; CMYK is out of scope.
- Full-resolution developed renders are required (the publish gate of plan 09 handles the wait).

## Steps

1. Shared: `PrintOutputSchema`, paper presets (A5, A4, US letter, 8×10, square 8×8), `trimBox`/`bleedBox` maths.
2. CLI: `print.ts` with the imposition and marks drawn as SVG over the page; 300 dpi images from the full renders.
3. Editor: Print settings panel in the inspector for the output; **Export PDF…** runs the CLI through the publish
   runner, shows progress in the queue, reveals the file.
4. A zine preset: A5 spreads, 3 mm bleed, marks on.

## Check

- The PDF opens in Preview at the trim size plus bleed; marks sit outside the trim; an untouched page's render is
  byte-identical to the web render at the same pixel size.
