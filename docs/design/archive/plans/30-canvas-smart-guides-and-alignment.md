WORKFLOW_PLAN [11.2](../WORKFLOW_PLAN.md#112-proposal) (W7). Size: 2 days. Depends on: 29.

**Goal.** Equal-spacing and size-match snapping, and an alignment toolbar for multi-selections with Tidy.

## Decisions

- Engine additions in `packages/shared/src/layouts/snap.ts`: equal spacing across three or more frames (snap when the
  gap being made equals the neighbours' gap, label "equal"), size match on resize (label "same width" / "same
  height"). Threshold and Cmd bypass unchanged.
- Toolbar on a multi-selection: align left / centre / right / top / middle / bottom, distribute horizontally /
  vertically, match width / height, **Tidy** (equal gaps on the current rows). One undo step each.
- **Fix spacing** hint: when a drop breaks the page's gutter rhythm (gap variance above a threshold), a small button
  in the floating toolbar runs Tidy on that row.

## Steps

1. Engine: `equalSpacingCandidates`, `sizeMatchCandidates` in `snap.ts`; `packages/shared/src/layouts/align.ts` with
   the align, distribute, match and tidy maths on frame records; tests (Tidy gaps within 1/1000).
2. Editor: `components/editor/AlignToolbar.tsx`; `setFrames` for the batch; the hint detection in `frameActions.ts`.
3. `CanvasGuides.tsx` draws the new guide kinds with labels and the distance badge.

## Check

- Tidy on a three-up row yields gaps within 1/1000 of each other (test); align and distribute tests; the renderer is
  byte-identical for untouched pages.
- Guides appear while dragging the middle of three frames.
