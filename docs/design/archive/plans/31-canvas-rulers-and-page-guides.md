WORKFLOW_PLAN [11.2](../WORKFLOW_PLAN.md#112-proposal) (W7). Size: 1 day. Depends on: 30, 03.

**Goal.** Rulers along the canvas in the template unit, drag-out guides stored on the page, a snap toggle in the
view menu and a "show guides while dragging" preference.

## Decisions

- `page.guides` (schema v8, plan 03): `{ axis, value }` with `value` a fraction of the page; the engine snaps to them.
- Rulers in inches or mm from the template; a drag from a ruler creates a guide; drag a guide back to the ruler to
  delete; double-click a guide to type a value; guides are not rendered by the shared renderer (editor only).
- `ui.canvas.snapEnabled` exists (the `bypass` flag); add `ui.canvas.showGuidesWhileDragging` persisted with the
  other prefs.

## Steps

1. `components/editor/Rulers.tsx`; `updatePage` adds, moves, removes guides (undoable).
2. `snap.ts` takes page guides as candidates (shared engine, labelled "guide").
3. View menu entries and the pref.

## Check

- A guide survives save and reload; snapping to it works; the renderer ignores it (snapshot unchanged).
