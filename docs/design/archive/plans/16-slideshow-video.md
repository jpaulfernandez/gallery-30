Status: done 2026-10-13, with these deviations. **Encoder** (decided before code): the static FFmpeg 6.0 of the PyPI package `ae-ffmpeg` 1.2.0 (Auto-Editor's build), LGPL v3: its metadata and the wheel's `LICENSE.txt` say LGPLv3, and the configure line embedded in the binary (read with `strings`, never executed) has `--enable-version3` and `--enable-videotoolbox` and no `--enable-gpl`, `--enable-nonfree`, libx264 or libx265 (libav* report "LGPL version 3 or later"). Pinned in `apps/editor/src-tauri/tools.toml` (compiled in, parsed with `toml` like `models.toml`): the macOS arm64 wheel (9,533,716 bytes, sha256 6f0a1a05…ca70) and x86_64 wheel (8,549,610 bytes, 8a8bfd3b…0a0c), the member path of the program in each and its own size and sha256; `commands/tools.rs` downloads (reqwest, progress `tools:progress`, cancel), checks both checksums, extracts with the system `/usr/bin/unzip` (wheels are zips; no zip crate added) through a temp file and installs `<appdata>/tools/ffmpeg/<version>/ffmpeg`; never bundled. Size: 3 days (one session for the decision and frames, one for encoding). Depends on: 13, 09.

**Goal.** An MP4 (1080p or 4K) of the pages or the picks with Ken Burns, cross-fades and optional audio, for
TikTok, Reels and YouTube.

## Decisions (first session)

- Encoder: prefer an LGPL ffmpeg build without x264 and encode with VideoToolbox on macOS (`h264_videotoolbox`),
  downloaded on demand like the models (pinned URL, bytes, SHA-256 in a `tools.toml`), never bundled. Record the
  choice and the licence check in the status line before writing code.
- The `slideshow` output variant: `{ source, order, secondsPerSlide, transition, motion, size, audioPath? }`.

## Steps

1. Frames: the renderer (in-app or Playwright, whichever 14 chose) renders each page at the output size; Ken Burns
   is a per-frame crop sequence computed in TS and rendered as still frames at the output fps (no video compositor).
2. Encoding: `commands/video.rs` spawns ffmpeg with an image sequence and the audio, progress parsed from stderr,
   cancel kills the process; runs in the queue.
3. UI: settings in the inspector, Export video… button, a preview of one transition.

## Check

- A 1080p MP4 plays on an iPhone; the frame count equals slides × seconds × fps; cancel leaves no partial file.
